# ⚡ QA Job Hunter - AI ATS Form Filler Chrome Extension (Unpacked)

AI-Powered 1-Click Form Filler for **Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Naukri, Ashby, BambooHR, SmartRecruiters** and any official company career portal. Powered by **Groq AI (qwen/qwen3.8-27b)** and pre-loaded with your verified Senior SDET / QA Automation Engineer profile data.

## 🚀 How to Install in Google Chrome (30 Seconds)

1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Go to the URL: `chrome://extensions` (or open Chrome Menu **⋮** -> **Extensions** -> **Manage Extensions**).
3. Toggle ON **"Developer mode"** in the top-right corner.
4. Click the **"Load unpacked"** button in the top-left corner.
5. Select the folder:
   ```
   c:\Users\SUSHANT KUMAR\Downloads\job finder\chrome-extension
   ```
6. That's it! The **"QA Job Hunter - AI ATS Form Filler Copilot"** icon will appear in your Chrome toolbar. Click the puzzle icon 🧩 and pin it 📌.

## 🎯 How It Works & Key Features

### 1. In-Page Floating AI Dock
When you open any job application page (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Naukri, etc.):
- A sleek floating widget automatically appears in the bottom-right corner: **`⚡ AutoFill with Groq AI`**.
- It is draggable and minimizable.
- Click **"AutoFill with Groq AI"** to instantly fill all fields with your data!

### 2. Keyboard Shortcut: `Alt + Shift + F`
On any active job application tab, press **`Alt + Shift + F`** on your keyboard to trigger instant AI form filling without touching the mouse!

### 3. Fields Automatically Mapped:
- **Full Name / First Name / Last Name**
- **Email & Phone Number** (`+91 6204507166`)
- **LinkedIn & GitHub URLs**
- **Current Company**: `Corecard India Pvt. Ltd`
- **Current Role**: `Software Development Engineer in Test (SDET-II)`
- **Total Experience**: `4.5 years`
- **Notice Period**: `30 days (Negotiable)`
- **Current CTC & Expected CTC**: `12,00,000 INR` / `18,00,000 INR`
- **Location, City, State, Country, Postal Code**: `Bengaluru, Karnataka, India (560100)`
- **Education**: Degree, University, Year of Graduation, GPA
- **Standard ATS Questions**:
  - Legally authorized to work? -> Automatically selects **Yes**
  - Require visa sponsorship? -> Automatically selects **No**
  - Gender / Veteran / Disability standard disclosures

### 4. Groq AI Custom Question Solver:
For open-ended questions like:
- *"Why do you want to join our company?"*
- *"Describe your experience with automated testing / ISO 8583 / payment gateways"*
- *"Explain your testing process and CI/CD workflow"*
- *"Cover Letter"*
The extension uses **Groq AI** (`qwen/qwen3.8-27b`) to generate an intelligent, logical 2 to 3 sentence answer tailored specifically to your 4.5+ years SDET experience and types it into the form.

### 5. 1-Click Local Sync (`http://localhost:5000`):
Open the extension popup and click **"🔄 Sync with Local QA Hunter (localhost:5000)"** anytime you update your resume or profile in the main web app to keep your extension 100% updated in real-time.
